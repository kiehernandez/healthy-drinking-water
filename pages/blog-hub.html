Learn & stay healthy

Why clean water is only the first step

Clean water keeps you alive. Healthy water — mineral-rich and alkaline — keeps you thriving. Read our simple guides to understand the difference.

1

Clean vs. Healthy Water

"Clean" means free of dirt and germs. "Healthy" means it also has minerals and a balanced pH that works with your body. We do both.

2

What Alkaline Water Does

Your body gets acidic from stress, sugary drinks, and pollution. Alkaline water helps balance pH, flush toxins, and give you magnesium for your heart.

3

7 Health Benefits

Weight loss, detoxification, pH balance, bone health, better hydration, heart health, and energy — one glass at a time.

4

Why Monthly Testing Matters

We test our water every month with a DOH-accredited lab so you know your family is drinking certified-safe water, every time.

5

Hydration Tips

Drink a glass when you wake up, before each meal, and before bed. Alkaline water tastes sweeter, so it's easier to drink more.

★

Simple Rule

Clean water keeps you alive. Healthy water keeps you thriving.
