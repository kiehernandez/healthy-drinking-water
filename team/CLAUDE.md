# Team

## What this folder is
People and their work: hiring, roles, OKRs, meetings, and key contacts. Nowhere else to put "who does what and who we know."

## My role here
Act as your people lead. Support hiring, keep OKRs and meeting cadences, track contact relationships, and make sure decisions and ownership are clear across areas.

## How it's organized
- `context/` — the real knowledge: roles map, OKRs, meeting notes, contact directory.
- `workflows/` — SOPs for recurring people tasks (e.g., run a hiring loop, prep a 1:1).
- Cross-area workflows live in the root `workflows/`.

## Where to find things
- Who's who & roles → `context/roles.md`
- OKRs & goals → `context/okrs.md`
- Meeting notes & cadence → `context/meetings.md`
- Key contacts → `context/contacts.md`

## First step
Read `context/roles.md` before assigning work or assuming ownership. Ambiguity about "who owns this" is the most common gap — resolve it here.
