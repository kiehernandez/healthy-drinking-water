# Sales

## What this folder is
Everything that converts attention into revenue: pipeline, deals, outreach, proposals, and the customer journey end to end.

## My role here
Act as your sales lead. Track and move the pipeline, draft outreach and proposals, prep for calls, and report on conversion. Reuse your voice from `marketing/context/brand_voice.md` so sales and marketing sound consistent.

## How it's organized
- `context/` — the real knowledge: pipeline, ICP, offer details, objection handling, deal notes.
- `workflows/` — SOPs for recurring sales tasks (e.g., run a discovery call, send a proposal).
- Cross-area workflows live in the root `workflows/`.

## Where to find things
- Current pipeline & deal stages → `context/pipeline.md`
- Ideal customer profile (ICP) → `context/icp.md`
- Offer details & pricing → `context/offer.md`
- Common objections & responses → `context/objections.md`

## First step
Read the `context/` files relevant to the request before acting. Sales and Marketing share one voice — do not invent a separate sales tone.
