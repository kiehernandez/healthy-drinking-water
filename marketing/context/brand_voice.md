# Brand Voice — Healthy Drinking Water Refilling Station

## Brand Identity
**Name:** Healthy Drinking Water Refilling Station  
**Tagline:** Your Family Deserves Clean, Safe and Healthy Drinking Water  
**Core Promise:** Rain or shine, we deliver quality mineral & alkaline water that helps you stay healthy, hydrated, and balanced.

## Tone & Personality
| Dimension | Our Voice |
|---|---|
| **Tone** | Warm, trustworthy, clear, science-backed but never jargon-heavy |
| **Energy** | Confident, caring, accessible — like a neighbor who knows water science |
| **Language** | Taglish (natural Filipino-English mix) — "Ang healthy water, hindi luxury, kailangan." |
| **Authority** | DOH-compliant, lab-tested, 24-level filtration — we prove it, don't just claim it |

## Words & Phrases

### ✅ Use These
- "Clean, safe, healthy" (the trilogy)
- "Mineral & Alkaline" — specific, not vague
- "DOH-compliant" / "Lab-certified" / "Monthly tested"
- "Pasa sa standards" — conversational proof
- "Sulit" — value without sounding cheap
- "Alagang pamilya" — care language
- "Rain or shine" — reliability

### ❌ Avoid These
- "Premium" (overused, means nothing)
- "State-of-the-art" (corporate speak)
- "Best in class" (unprovable)
- Medical claims beyond what lab results show
- Pure English — feels distant

## Voice Examples

**Social post:**  
"Hindi lang tubig 'to. Mineral & alkaline, 24-level filtered, DOH-certified. Monthly tested, pasa lahat. Ang pamilya mo, deserve nito. 💧"

**Educational snippet:**

## Proof Points (Always Lead With These)
1. **DOH-compliant** — Sanitary Permit, Sanitary Clearance for delivery vehicle, Barangay/Mayor's/DTI permits
2. **Monthly lab testing** — Boyer Environmental (DOH-accredited), passes *all* parameters: physical, chemical, bacteriological, radiological
3. **24-level filtration** — Micro Filtration + UV Sterilizer + Mineral & Alkaline, 10,000 GPD capacity
4. **Since 2020** — 4+ years operating, consistent "PASSED" results
5. **Location trust** — 6797 Tramo St., San Dionisio, Parañaque (near Las Piñas boundary, INC Church landmark)
