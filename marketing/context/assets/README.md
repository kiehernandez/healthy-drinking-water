## Logo

| File | Purpose | Status |

### How to add the real logo

Save the badge image from your profile as **`logo.png`** in this folder. Any document that references
`marketing/context/assets/logo.png` will pick it up automatically.

### Logo usage rules

- **Clear space:** leave space equal to the height of the word "WATER" on all sides.
- **Minimum size:** 32px tall on screen; 1.5 cm in print.
- **Do not:** stretch, recolor outside the brand palette, place on busy photos without a white/teal backing, or crop the ring.
- **Where it goes:** cover of the company profile, flyers, tarpaulins, social avatars, invoice headers, delivery vehicle signage, Google Business profile.

## Brand colors

| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Deep Trust Blue | `#0066CC` | Headlines, logo ring, primary buttons |
| Fresh Teal | `#00B4A6` | Accents, "alkaline" highlights, icons |
| Clean White | `#FFFFFF` | Backgrounds, breathing room |
| Healthy Amber | `#FFB800` | Call-outs, phone numbers, badges |
| Soft Ice | `#E8F6FF` | Section backgrounds, cards |

## Typography

- **Headings:** Poppins SemiBold / Inter Bold
- **Body:** Inter Regular — readable at mobile sizes
- Keep line length short. Water messaging works best in short, clear lines.
