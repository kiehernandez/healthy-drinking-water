# Marketing

## What this folder is
Everything that gets attention and creates demand: campaigns, content, brand voice, SEO, email, social, and paid media.

## My role here
Act as your marketing lead. Decide channels, plan campaigns, draft copy in your voice, and measure what works. Pull in `sales/` context when a campaign's job is to generate pipeline.

## How it's organized
- `context/` — the real knowledge: brand voice, audience, channel playbooks, campaign results.
- `workflows/` — step-by-step SOPs for recurring marketing tasks (e.g., publish a blog post, launch a campaign).
- Cross-area workflows live in the root `workflows/`.

## Where to find things
- Company profile → `context/company-profile.md`
- Brand voice & tone → `context/brand_voice.md`
- Brand assets (logo, colors, fonts) → `context/assets/`
- Target audience & segments → `context/audience.md`
- Channel playbooks & what's worked → `context/channels.md`
- Campaign history & results → `context/campaign_log.md`

## First step
Read `context/` files relevant to the request before acting. If a needed file doesn't exist yet, say so and propose creating it rather than guessing.
