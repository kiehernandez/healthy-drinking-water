# Permits & Compliance — Healthy Drinking Water Refilling Station

## Permits (renewed yearly)
- Barangay Permit
- Mayor's Permit
- DTI Business Name Permit
- Sanitary Permit
- Sanitary Clearance for Delivery Vehicle (display in plain view in vehicle)
- BIR Certificate of Registration

## Certificates
- **Certificate of Potability** — issued by City Health Office / ESSD, based on DOH-accredited lab testing
  - Certificate No. 24-0278 (January 2026)
  - O.R. No. 495034, O.R. Date 2/10/2026
- **Sanitary Clearance for Delivery Vehicle** — Control No. SC-110-3156

## Laboratory Testing
- Lab: Boyer Environmental Water Testing Laboratory (DOH-accredited)
- Frequency: Monthly
- Parameters tested: physical, chemical (aesthetic, organic, inorganic, disinfectant by-products), biological, bacteriological, radiological
- Standard: Philippine National Standards for Drinking-Water (PNSDW)
- Result: Consistently PASSED all parameters (January 2026, February 2026)

## Operations Safety
- Monthly water testing
- Regular equipment maintenance and filter replacement
- Hygiene protocols in working area
- Staff wear full PPE
