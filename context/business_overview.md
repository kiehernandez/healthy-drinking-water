## What the business is

- **Name:** Healthy Drinking Water Refilling Station
- **What we do:** Refill/concentrate and deliver healthy drinking water (mineral & alkaline) door-to-door
- **Location:** 6797 Tramo Street, San Dionisio, Parañaque City (near Las Piñas Manuyo Uno boundary, beside Iglesia ni Cristo Church)
- **Since:** September 2020

## Offer

- Mineral water — purifies + adds essential minerals (calcium, magnesium, potassium, sodium); pH balance, toxin flushing, hydration, osteoporosis support
- Alkaline water — mineral + alkaline; 7 health benefits (weight loss, detox, pH balance, bone health, hydration, heart health, energy/endurance)
- Colored drinking water containers — Php 200/pc
- Delivery: Smart 0947-623 0882 | Globe 0927-048-5948 — door-to-door, rain or shine

## Quality standard

- 24-level filtration + UV sterilizer; 10,000 GPD capacity
- DOH-compliant; monthly lab testing (Boyer Environmental, DOH-accredited); Certificate of Potability renewed yearly
- Sanitary Clearance for delivery vehicle (displayed in vehicle)
- Barangay / Mayor's / DTI / Sanitary / BIR permits renewed yearly
