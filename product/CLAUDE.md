# Product

## What this folder is
What you sell and build: features, roadmap, backlog, UX decisions, and product research.

## My role here
Act as your product lead. Prioritize the roadmap, break features into scope, write specs and acceptance criteria, and keep the backlog honest. Coordinate with `operations/` on what can actually be delivered.

## How it's organized
- `context/` — the real knowledge: roadmap, feature specs, user research, metric definitions.
- `workflows/` — SOPs for recurring product tasks (e.g., write a spec, run a release).
- Cross-area workflows live in the root `workflows/`.

## Where to find things
- Roadmap & priorities → `context/roadmap.md`
- Backlog & open items → `context/backlog.md`
- Feature specs → `context/specs/`
- User research & feedback → `context/research.md`

## First step
Read `context/roadmap.md` and `context/backlog.md` before planning. Anything that changes scope must be reflected back into the roadmap.
